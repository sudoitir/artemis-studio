### Fixed

- **Pause auto-refresh on every screen, at once** ([b9839ca5](https://github.com/sudoitir/artemis-studio/commit/b9839ca56a0b48f8fa772d1c1e604d08066117cc))

  Pausing auto-refresh now stops every periodic refetch on the screen,
  including those of runtime plugins and any hook with a plain interval.
  Before, only hooks that wrapped their interval in a Studio helper paused,
  and even those fetched once more after the pause.

  Pause is now enforced once, at the query client, through TanStack Query's
  focusManager: the tab counts as focused only while it is visible and
  refreshing is not paused, and every interval checks that before it fires.
  Plugin authors need do nothing. A request that fails while paused retries
  after resume. ADR-0118.
