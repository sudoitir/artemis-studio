## 2026.09.52 — 2026-09-28

### Added

- **Let DiagramView offer inserts on arrows and actions on boxes** ([2f9fbb05](https://github.com/sudoitir/artemis-studio/commit/2f9fbb0599f2a87f7c03c8d84499c369a6f118ab))

  A plugin can now let people change the structure it draws from the diagram
  itself, while keeping every rule about what may go where:

  - Mark an edge `insertable` and pass `insertChoices` with `onInsert`. The edge
    shows a "+" that offers the choices, and Insert on the box it leads to
    offers the same from the keyboard.
  - Pass `nodeActions` with `onNodeAction`. A box's actions open from its "⋯",
    a right-click, or Shift+F10. An action that does not apply stays listed,
    disabled, with its reason.
  - Zoom in and zoom out sit beside Fit, and `height` also takes a CSS length,
    so `'100%'` fills a resizable panel.

  The diagram still never changes its nodes: it reports the choice and the
  caller applies it. Without the new props it looks and behaves as before.
  Fitting the view after a layout no longer closes an open menu; only the
  user's own pans do. ADR-0120 amends ADR-0117.
