## 2026.09.60 — 2026-09-29

### Fixed

- **Show a cluster's depth and consumers as totals, not averages** ([ca5be412](https://github.com/sudoitir/artemis-studio/commit/ca5be41225161beab181e8363bea1a3e0c2a93ac))

  The cluster-wide Depth and Consumers charts now show the cluster's total:
  at each bucket's end, the sum of every queue's latest sample on every node.
  They previously averaged all samples in a bucket, so a cluster holding
  10,000 messages across 50 queues read about 200. A queue on the slow sweep
  now counts in every bucket until its own sampling interval lapses, and a
  deleted queue stops counting after that interval. The cluster chart is
  labelled "Depth (total)" and has no bucket peak, since a total's peak is
  not the sum of its queues' peaks. Queue charts are unchanged.

- **Count every increment in a counter's rate, even at a fine step** ([7f141864](https://github.com/sudoitir/artemis-studio/commit/7f1418640d480f7c8e1b74a36c298c525aa01971))

  A queue's rate charts (messages added, acknowledged, expired) no longer
  read 0 msg/s for a busy queue at the 15-minute range. Each bucket now
  counts what arrived since the queue's previous sample, not only the change
  between samples inside the bucket, so a bucket holding one sample reads
  the real rate and nothing added between two buckets is lost. The first
  bucket of a range with no earlier sample is left out instead of shown as 0.
  Per-node series still add up to the total.
