### Added

- **List the clusters a caller may read through StudioInfo** ([34236a39](https://github.com/sudoitir/artemis-studio/commit/34236a39d53862cfd45877973cbde0860592b592))

  StudioInfo.clusters() maps the id of every cluster the current caller
  holds cluster:read on to its display name, ordered by name. A plugin can
  now let its documents name clusters and store their ids; clusterName(id)
  already gave the reverse.
