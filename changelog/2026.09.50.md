## 2026.09.50 — 2026-09-28

### Added

- **Add a read-only DiagramView for plugins** ([79fd7827](https://github.com/sudoitir/artemis-studio/commit/79fd7827b0bd81cee7b17bb201e29a4d9131e94e))

  Plugins can now draw boxes and arrows in Studio's style: import DiagramView from
  @artemis-studio/plugin-sdk and pass plain nodes and edges. It is laid out for you
  (layered, off the main thread), and a node or edge label change never moves anything.

  - selectedId and onSelect tie it to a detail pane.
  - A node with state 'error' or 'warning' shows the word on the card and reads its reason out.
  - The diagram is one tab stop: arrow keys move between nodes, Enter selects, and the
    selection is announced.
  - It is read-only: nothing drags or connects.

  See ADR-0117 and the plugin guide.
