### Added

- **Redraw the Studio mark as a drawn bow** ([1f22b4cc](https://github.com/sudoitir/artemis-studio/commit/1f22b4ccca795e0b5e1961585d946afe51fbf995))

  The crescent bow now reads as a bow. Its string is drawn back and it looses a
  real arrow, with a head and a nock, flying up and to the right. The earlier
  version had neither string nor arrowhead, so the arrow read as a pin or a
  toggle. A faint ring completes the moon, a thin rim catches the light along the
  bow, and a dark gap separates the arrow from the bow where they cross. The
  drawing is centred on its own bounding box, and it stays legible down to a
  16 px browser tab on light and dark chrome.
